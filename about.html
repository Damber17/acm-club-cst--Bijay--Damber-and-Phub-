ABOUT ACM CST

About ACM CST

A student community dedicated to technology, innovation, learning and collaboration at the College of Science and Technology.

WHO WE ARE

More than a club. A community.

ACM CST is a student community at the College of Science and Technology, Royal University of Bhutan.

The chapter brings together students who are interested in computing, technology and innovation.

Through workshops, competitions, projects, meetups and collaborative activities, ACM CST provides students with opportunities to develop practical skills and explore new technologies.

Our Community

We believe that students learn best when they work together. ACM CST encourages students to share knowledge, experiment with ideas and support one another while developing their technical and professional skills.
